figurenumbers 0.1.0 (development)
=================================

Initial version with the following functions:

* `generate_math_problems()` generates a numbered sequence of arithmetic
  problems (addition, subtraction, or multiplication), one problem per
  target answer.
* `numberGridGrob()` creates a grid grob of a 10x10 grid of (small) circles
  representing the numbers 1 to 100 (in reading order).
* `gridsGrob()` arranges several blank `numberGridGrob()` panels on a
  page, for printing sheets to hand-sketch candidate puzzle pictures on.
* `problemsGrob()` creates a grid grob laying out arithmetic problems
  (as generated by `generate_math_problems()`) in long-form "stacked" style.
* `puzzleGrob()` creates a grid grob of a complete puzzle page:
  arithmetic problems on one side and the number grid on the other side
  of a landscape page.
* `read_puzzles()` reads puzzle definitions from a YAML file,
  defaulting to the puzzles bundled with this package.
